Pad-Ga-Pao

Description

Pad Krapow is a popular Thai dish consisting of meat stir-fried with chili, garlic, and holy basil, resulting in a fragrant and bold-flavored meal that is served alongside hot steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 200–400 grams of minced pork
  • 1 bunch of holy basil, or 50 grams
  • Garlic and red Jinda chilies, pounded coarsely to your preference.
  • 1–2 tbsp vegetable oil (optional, if stir-frying dry)
  • 1–3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp seasoning sauce or light soy sauce
  • ½–1 tbsp fish sauce
  • ½–1 tsp sugar
  • A splash of dark sweet soy sauce (for color)
